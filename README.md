# 🇺🇸 Civics Quest

A gamified practice app for the **2025 USCIS Naturalization Civics Test** — all **128 official questions** (M-1778) in a single, self-contained HTML file. No server, no install, works offline.

**[▶ Open the app](https://nilsno.github.io/civics-quest/)** — or download `index.html` and open it in any browser.

## The real test (rules baked into the app)

- Oral test: **20 of 128** questions asked
- **12 correct = pass** — the officer stops at 12 ✓ or 9 ✗
- **65/20 rule:** applicants 65+ with 20+ years as an LPR study the 20 ⭐ questions and need 6/10

## Features

- 🎴 **Smart flashcards** — unseen and weak cards come back more often; self-grade 😅 Nope / 🤔 Almost / 💪 Got it
- 🏛️ **Test Sim** — mirrors the real test, including early stopping, then shows a pass/fail verdict like test day
- ⭐ **65/20 mode** — practice just the 20 starred questions
- 🎯 **Weak-spot drills** — one tap to focus on your worst cards
- 📊 **Mastery stats** — per-question tracking (new / weak / solid), overall mastery meter, 🔥 daily streak
- 💬 Encouragements — because confidence is the whole game
- 📱 **Mobile-first** — big touch targets, dark theme, saves progress on-device (localStorage)

## Notes

- Answers to election-dependent questions (Speaker of the House, state governor, etc.) are set to current officeholders — check [uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates](https://www.uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates) near the interview date.
- Question data sourced from the official USCIS [128 Civics Questions and Answers (2025 version)](https://www.uscis.gov/sites/default/files/document/questions-and-answers/2025-Civics-Test-128-Questions-and-Answers.pdf).
